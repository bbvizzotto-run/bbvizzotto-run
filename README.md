### Research & Teaching Focus

I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science in the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul (**UFRGS**) with a research fellowship background at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (**KIT**) in Germany. At **Unipampa**, I teach undergraduate engineering courses and lead computational infrastructure workflows at the *Laboratório de Ópticas e Comunicações (LAOC)*.

* **Research Areas:** Video Coding Standards (`H.264/AVC`, `H.266/VVC`), Reinforcement Learning for Adaptive Buffer Control, Vision Transformers, Image Processing, and Network Protocol Architectures, .
* **Courses Taught:** Operating Systems, Computer Networks, Communication Network Infrastructure & Design, and Telecommunications Engineering.
* **Pedagogical Engineering:** Building interactive web simulations, Packet Tracer labs, and self-hosted open-source educational tools to turn dense theoretical concepts into hands-on visual experiences.
